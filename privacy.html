Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026.

DOMI publisher ("the App") is a personal tool used by its owner to publish their own content.

Data we access

When the owner authorizes the App with TikTok, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name) and may upload videos that the owner chooses to publish.

How data is used and stored

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and used only to publish content the owner has approved. Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings and delete the locally stored token file.
